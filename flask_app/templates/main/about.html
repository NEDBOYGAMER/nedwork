What is nedwork?

nedwork is a personal hobby website: a handful of small tools and utilities I built because I needed them, kept online so my friends can use them too. It is not a company, not a product, and nothing here costs money.

This page is the notice board — every change to the site lands in the changelog below, and the guide further down explains how to find your way around.

    Getting around

    nedwork stays deliberately flat — here's all you need to know.

    01

    Everything starts at the home grid

    Every tool nedwork has lives on the home page as a tile. Click a tile, the tool opens — nothing buried in menus. Middle-click to open one in a new tab.

    02

    Lost? Come back here

    This page doubles as the map: the changelog lists every new tool and every change, so when something looks different, you'll read it here first.

    03

    Make it yours

    Open the settings to switch dark or light mode, pick any accent color you like, or turn the background grid off. Choices apply to the whole site, not just this page.

    Privacy & data

    No lawyer wrote this — I did. If something is unclear, ask me directly.

    38 → 24

    Passwords

    Let's say your password is 38. The hashing algorithm — in reality far more complex — simply multiplies it, so it becomes 24. That 24 is what gets saved on the server. When you log in again and type your password, the same happens again: 24 equals 24, so you're in. But whoever sees the 24 on the server has no way of knowing whether your password was 38, 46, 124 or 241 — they only ever see the result, never the input. And the better the hashing, the harder that gets. So nobody, not even I, can see the password you typed.

    It is of course a lot more complex than multiplying — curious? Read about cryptographic hash functions on wikipedia.

    This is a hobby project and only maintained by me. So, for your own safety: use a password you didn't use anywhere else — but also not one that's too easy, because I don't want just anyone to be able to enter this website.

    Your data

    Your data is saved on my server — and nowhere else. No Google, no Amazon, no Microsoft, no ad networks, no analytics. Nobody else can get to it, and I don't send it anywhere.

    So as long as you use the website normally and as intended, there is nothing to worry about.

    Backups & mistakes

    Of course, since this is a personal project things can go wrong on my side. But I do have a backup server which will restore any data lost.

    If you delete something on your own though, I won't restore it by default — I could, but doing so would wipe the newer things other people saved in the meantime. Maybe I'll still revert or help out if you ask, but I don't promise: what you delete stays your responsibility.
